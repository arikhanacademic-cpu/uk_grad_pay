How to read this

Spike height is the median annual pay of UK-domiciled first-degree graduates in the 2022–23 tax year, from the Department for Education’s LEO graduate outcomes data. Heights use one scale for every subject, so switching degree shows real differences.

The career steps compare three different graduating classes measured in the same tax year (graduated 2020–21, 2018–19 and 2016–17), not one group followed over time.

A missing spike means the course was too small for the government to publish earnings. Pay reflects where graduates work (London salaries are higher), their backgrounds and entry grades, not only the teaching. Accounting and finance are counted inside Business and management in this data. Distance and multi-site providers (the Open University, University of London, BPP, University of Law, UCEM) are left off the map.

Spike positions are approximate main-campus locations.
